## Template Structure

Every brief includes:

1. **Keyword Data** — Volume, KD, intent, parent topic
2. **SERP Analysis** — What's ranking, content gaps, word count targets
3. **Audience & Angle** — Who reads this, what they actually want
4. **Outline** — H2/H3 structure with talking points per section
5. **SEO Requirements** — Target keywords, internal links, meta specs
6. **Conversion Elements** — CTAs, lead magnets, affiliate slots
7. **Quality Checklist** — E-E-A-T signals, readability, technical SEO
