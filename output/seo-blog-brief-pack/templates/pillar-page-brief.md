## 8. SEO Technical Specs

**Target Word Count:** 4,500-7,000 words
**Heading Structure:** H1 (1) → H2 (12) → H3 (30-40) → H4 (minimal)
**Images:** 15-25 (diagrams, screenshots, charts, photos) — all compressed, WebP, alt text
**Videos:** 2-3 embeds (YouTube/Vimeo — own or licensed)
**Interactive Elements:** 2-3 (calculator, quiz, template preview, table sort)

**Schema Markup:**
- `Article` + `WebPage` + `BreadcrumbList`
- `TableOfContents` (custom)
- `HowTo` (for framework section)
- `FAQPage` (FAQ section)
- `ItemList` (tools table)

- LCP < 2.5s
