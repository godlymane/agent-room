Stop guessing what to write. Use these 12 SEO brief templates instead. 📝

I've watched content teams burn 2-3 hours per article just figuring out *what to write*. Keyword in hand, they stare at blank docs: What's the search intent? What structure ranks? What headers, FAQs, internal links? By the time they outline, momentum is dead.

So I built an **SEO Blog Brief Template Pack** — 12 Markdown templates that transform a target keyword into a complete, writer-ready content brief.

## What's Inside

### 12 Brief Templates

| # | Template | Best For |

| 01 | **Pillar/Cluster Hub Brief** | Core topic pages, topic clusters |

### Each Brief Includes

- **Keyword intelligence block** — primary/secondary keywords, volume, difficulty, intent
- **SERP analysis template** — top 10 competitors, content gaps, angle opportunities
- **Structured outline** — H2/H3 hierarchy with word count targets
- **FAQ schema-ready questions** — from PAA, forums, support tickets
- **Internal link map** — existing assets to link, anchor text suggestions
- **Writer brief** — tone, audience, CTA, style notes, examples to reference
- **Content scorecard** — SEO checklist for editor review

## Why Markdown?

- Lives in your repo beside content
- Version-controlled briefs — track strategy evolution
- Works in Notion, Obsidian, VS Code, any editor
- CI/CD friendly — lint briefs in PR reviews
- Zero vendor lock-in

## Quick Start

```bash
# 1. Pick your brief type
cp templates/02-how-to-tutorial-brief.md briefs/target-keyword.md

# 2. Fill keyword intel (volume, intent, difficulty)
# 3. Run SERP analysis (template guides you)
# 4. Build outline from competitor gaps
# 4. Add FAQs from PAA + support tickets
# 5. Map internal links from your content audit
# 6. Hand to writer — they start drafting, not researching
```
